    <h2 class="reveal">Three initiatives, working together.</h2>
    <p class="reveal">We don't treat victim support and policy change as separate tracks — the cases we hear directly shape the reforms we push for.</p>
    <ol class="log reveal">
    <li><span class="tag">01 — ADVOCATE</span><p>Push for stronger platform protocols: a direct line to Seoul law enforcement and the platforms themselves, with regular, mandatory checks on flagged content instead of relying on victims to report after the fact.</p></li>
    <li><span class="tag">02 — SUPPORT</span><p>Build a free victim support hub connecting people affected by deepfake abuse with legal guidance and confidential emotional support.</p></li>
    <li><span class="tag">03 — DETECT</span><p>Develop recommendations for platform-level safeguards — watermarking standards, faster takedown pathways — using patterns from real reports to show regulators where current systems fall short.</p></li>
    </ol>
